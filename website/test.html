📋 Instructions & Tips

Instructions:

  • You will be presented with visual pattern recognition problems
  • Each problem contains example input-output pairs to help you understand the pattern
  • Your task is to identify the underlying rule and apply it to new test cases
  • Use the color palette and drawing tools to create your answers
  • After solving all problems, describe what each problem reminds you of in the real world
  • Once you submit your answers, you cannot return to edit them
  • You can save your progress at any time and return later, but please finish the formal test within 120 hours.

Tips:

  • Study the examples carefully to understand the transformation pattern
  • Look for spatial relationships, color changes, and geometric patterns
  • 🔍 Click on any example to enlarge it - easier to see details in small grids
  • Take your time - there's no time limit
  • More compensation for more correct answers!
